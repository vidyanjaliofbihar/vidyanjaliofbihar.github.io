About Us

Vidyanjali of Bihar is a registered public charitable trust based in Rohtas, Bihar, committed to promoting quality education, strengthening schools, empowering teachers and supporting inclusive community development.

Our focus areas include educational awareness, school leadership, teacher development, digital and vocational skills, health and social welfare, women and child development, and environmental awareness.

We work towards creating meaningful educational opportunities and supporting disadvantaged communities through learning, collaboration and charitable initiatives.

Our Vision & Mission

Guided by the charitable and educational objectives of our Trust.

🌟

Our Vision

To build an inclusive and empowered society where quality education, equal opportunities and community participation enable every individual to achieve their potential.

🎯

Our Mission

To promote quality education, strengthen school leadership, empower teachers, support underprivileged communities and advance social development through awareness, training, collaboration and charitable initiatives.
