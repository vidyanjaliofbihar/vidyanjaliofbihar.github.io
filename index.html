About Us

Vidyanjali of Bihar is an organization working towards transformation through education and social upliftment. We aim to help create opportunities, strengthen schools, support educators and contribute to a brighter future for children and communities.

Our focus includes educational awareness, school leadership, teacher empowerment and community participation, aligned with the broader goals of inclusive and quality education.
